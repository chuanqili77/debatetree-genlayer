Open a new debate

Anyone may open a debate. You become its creator and the only one who may close it.

Add an argument

Flawed arguments subtract 1 from their own side. Post only claims your evidence actually supports.

How it works

  1. Open a debate with a topic and description.
  2. Post arguments FOR or AGAINST, each with an evidence link.
  3. Validators fetch the evidence and judge soundness: SOUND +2, UNVERIFIED 0, FLAWED −1.
  4. The creator closes the debate once at least 2 arguments are assessed; the higher total wins.

Limits

  • A verdict records the evidence judgment at assessment time; a later change to the page does not rewrite history.
  • Unreachable evidence leaves an argument pending; it can be assessed again later.
  • Closing clears nothing: assessed arguments, totals, and the winner stay on chain.
  • All writes use nominal Studionet accounting units; no real assets are held or transferred.
